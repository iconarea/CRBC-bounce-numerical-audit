## Covariant-action audit

The tested local thermal Hagedorn production route does not yield the required
conversion at the controlled transition scale. A canonical scalar with a
conformal pressureless-sector coupling supplies a covariant energy-transfer
term, but the tested potential families cannot both keep the pressure bounded
and sustain the transfer. A beyond-Horndeski construction reconstructs a
supplied background and therefore does not predict the transition.

A kinetic-dependent derivative coupling produces the desired background-level
descent in the proxy equations. It is not yet a covariant realization: direct
variation indicates additional terms of order tens relative to the proxy near
the bounce. Accordingly, this release does **not** claim a covariant action,
DHOST degeneracy, ghost freedom, gradient stability, or EFT control for that
candidate.
